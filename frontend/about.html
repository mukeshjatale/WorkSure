About WorkSure

Home help that people can trust

WorkSure connects households with skilled, verified professionals for everyday home jobs, from a dripping tap to a full repaint.

Our mission

Make it simple to find a reliable professional, know the price before you book, and see honest feedback from people who have used their services.

How we keep standards high

  • Professionals are checked and verified before customers can book them
  • Reviews come only from completed bookings
  • Every booking keeps a history of status changes

For customers

Search by service, area and price. Pick a time that fits the professional's working hours, and track the booking until the job is done. You can cancel a booking that is still pending, or an accepted one at least 24 hours before it starts.

For professionals

Create your profile, list your services with your own prices, set your working hours, and accept or decline requests. Once a job is completed, your earnings summary updates automatically.

Payments

WorkSure does not take payments yet. Customers and professionals agree on payment directly. Earnings shown in your dashboard are based on the agreed prices of completed jobs.
